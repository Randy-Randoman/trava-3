## SLIDE 3: What If Planning Was the Fun Part?

TRAVA is a visual trip planning canvas — powered by AI, built for groups.

Tell the AI what you want. It builds a visual board: flights, hotels, activities, transport, local spots, tips — your whole trip.

Share a link with your group — no sign-up needed. Everyone sees the same board.

Discuss, rearrange, decide together. Click to book.

Planning creates anticipation. TRAVA makes it visual, collaborative, exciting.

## SLIDE 4: Why Visual?

[Left side: screenshot of a ChatGPT text list output for "plan a trip to Lisbon"]
[Right side: screenshot of a TRAVA board with visual cards]

A list says "Hotel A, €120." A card with a rooftop pool photo makes you feel the trip.

Groups can't align on text — "which one was the second hotel again?" On a board, you point at the same thing.

A trip isn't linear. A list is. Organize by day, by neighborhood, by vibe — everyone plans differently.

Planning should feel like the start of the adventure, not homework.

## SLIDE 6: The Bigger Vision

Phase 1 (now)
Our agent + our canvas.
Full product. Get traction, validate.

Phase 2: Bring Your Own Agent
Any AI agent can push results to TRAVA boards via API/MCP.
We shift from "AI travel app" to "the visual layer for AI travel."
Our token costs drop to near zero. Distribution opens to every agent in the ecosystem.

Phase 3: Agentic Booking
Users click "Book" on the board, the agent handles everything.
Conversion jumps. The board becomes the booking interface.

Phase 1 is a product. Phase 2 is a platform. Phase 3 is the default interface for AI-planned travel.
